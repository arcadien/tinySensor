**Status:** excluded
**Exclusion ref:** STYLE-LAYOUT-001

### Finding 2
**Type:** Coherence — circular dependency (C4, in substance)
**Requirements:** PLAT-POWER-004, FUNC-MAIN-001
**Issue:** PLAT-POWER-004 says "each environment copy ([FUNC-MAIN-001]) is its own transmission", so it cannot be read without FUNC-MAIN-001, which depends on PLAT-POWER-004. Declaring the dependency would make a cycle; TEXT-BACKREF-001 does not cover this pair. State the transmission without FUNC-MAIN-001's copy concept (each frame copy an encoder sends on its own), or drop FUNC-MAIN-001's dependency on PLAT-POWER-004.
**Status:** fixed

### Finding 3
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirement:** PLAT-POWER-004
**Issue:** For an X10 frame the LED goes off "after its last bit" and "the 30 ms come after the 40 ms gap of its last copy"; whether the LED goes off before or after that gap is open. The code (`TRANSMIT_WITH_LED` around `Send()`) turns it off after the gap.
**Status:** fixed

### Finding 4
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirement:** TECH-SERIAL-002
**Issue:** The values are floats (`Measurements`) passed as `uint16_t`: humidity and pressure are truncated toward zero, and a negative temperature × 10 converted to `uint16_t` is undefined behaviour in C++. The requirement says "unsigned decimal" without the conversion, and nothing for negative temperatures. The logging path is built by no environment (TECH-SERIAL-002 is draft).
**Status:** fixed
