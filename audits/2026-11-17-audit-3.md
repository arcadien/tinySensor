## Findings

### Finding 1
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirements:** FUNC-LACROSSE-007, FUNC-DECODE-003
**Issue:** The luminosity frame carries the units, tens and hundreds digits of the lux value in 3 nibbles. The BH1750 reads up to 65535 lx, and no range, clamp or overflow rule is given above 999 lx. FUNC-DECODE-003 decodes `d3*100 + d2*10 + d1`, and the decoder tests (`lacrosseDecoderTest.cpp` lines 135, 343, 385; `rflinkFormatterTest.cpp` line 172) expect 1000 lux to round-trip. That only works if the hundreds nibble holds 10 (0xA), which is not a decimal digit.
**Status:** fixed

### Finding 2
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirements:** FUNC-SENSOR-001, TECH-SENSOR-002, FUNC-OREGON-008, FUNC-LACROSSE-006
**Issue:** FUNC-SENSOR-001 reads "the pressure" with no unit. TECH-SENSOR-002 returns Pa; the Oregon and Lacrosse frames carry hPa. The Pa → hPa conversion and its rounding or truncation are stated nowhere.
**Status:** fixed

### Finding 3
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirement:** FUNC-OREGON-007
**Issue:** Humidity is sent as two digits, 0–99 %. The requirement gives no rounding rule for a fractional reading and no behaviour at 100 % or more, which a BME280 can report. FUNC-LACROSSE-005 has an explicit clamp.
**Status:** fixed

### Finding 4
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirement:** FUNC-OREGON-005
**Issue:** Temperature is sent as three digits (tens, units, tenths). The requirement gives no rounding rule to tenths (e.g. 21.47 or 21.55 °C) and no behaviour at 100 °C or more. FUNC-LACROSSE-004 clamps to ±99 °C.
**Status:** fixed

### Finding 5
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirement:** FUNC-X10-003
**Issue:** "The command (on, off, bright, dim) is the rest of byte 2" doesn't give each command's bit values, so byte 2 can't be checked from the text alone.
**Status:** open

### Finding 6
**Type:** Coherence — overlap (C1)
**Requirements:** FUNC-DECODE-007, UI-DECODE-001
**Issue:** FUNC-DECODE-007 says a page is laid out per UI-DECODE-001, then defines the layout itself: 6 big 16×32 cells, a side column (8×16, columns 12–15) with the identifier on row 0 and humidity or pressure on row 2, and the waiting text on row 1. UI-DECODE-001 defines the same structure. It's unclear which requirement owns the geometry and which owns the field content and redraw scheduling.
**Status:** open

### Finding 7
**Type:** Coherence — architecture coherence (C5)
**Requirements:** TECH-HAL-001, FUNC-X10-005, FUNC-SENSOR-003
**Issue:** Three `docs/architecture.md` traceability rows lag behind their requirement:
- TECH-HAL-001 lists Hal / Attiny84aHal / TestHal_ / BMx280, but the requirement constrains the components that take a `Hal*`: OregonV3, LacrosseWS7000, x10rf, SoftSerial, Ds18b20 and LightSensor;
- FUNC-X10-005 says "repeated rf_repeats times with 40 ms inter-repeat cooldown", while the requirement fixes two copies per cycle and keeps the LED lit across both;
- FUNC-SENSOR-003 omits that nothing is sent while the battery is low, and the 150 ms wait before reading.
**Status:** fixed

### Finding 8
**Type:** Coherence — architecture coherence (C5), dependency injection
**Requirement:** TECH-DECODE-002
**Issue:** In the Dependency Injection Map, the Receiver's `ReceiverHal*` is a compile-time typedef of `AtmegaReceiverHal` (or `TestReceiverHal` in tests), not an abstract interface. `docs/architecture.md` documents this as an intentional static interface (no vtable). Proposal: add an exclusion.
**Status:** excluded
**Exclusion ref:** RECEIVER-STATIC-HAL-001

### Finding 9
**Type:** Traceability — tag validity (T2)
**Requirement:** TECH-SENSOR-001 (draft)
**Issue:** The 4 tests in `emitter/test/test_ds18b20/ds18b20Test.cpp` (lines 67, 80, 95, 110) are tagged with TECH-SENSOR-001, which went back to draft in 116db45. Plugin 0.16.0 now warns on tags citing a draft. Options: keep them and add an exclusion until the DS18B20 work re-validates it, or untag them.
**Status:** excluded
**Exclusion ref:** DRAFT-TAG-DS18B20-001
