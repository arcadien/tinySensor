### Finding 33
**Type:** Coherence — semantic overlap
**Requirements:** FUNC-BATTERY-001, TECH-HAL-003
**Issue:** FUNC-BATTERY-001 specifies the `ComputeVccMv()` formula (inputs, computation, return value). TECH-HAL-003 also defines `ComputeVccMv` as a non-virtual concrete method on `Hal`. The behavioral specification and the structural specification describe the same method from different angles without a clear boundary, making it possible to satisfy each individually while leaving an inconsistency between them.
**Status:** open

### Finding 34
**Type:** Coherence — semantic overlap
**Requirements:** TECH-HAL-004, PLAT-POWER-003
**Issue:** Both requirements describe register-level power-reduction operations on the same registers (DDRA, DDRB, PORTA, PORTB, PRR). TECH-HAL-004 covers constructor behavior (`UseLessPowerAsPossible`); PLAT-POWER-003 covers `sleep()` pre/post. They do not clarify whether the two operations are additive, idempotent, or sequential.
**Status:** open

### Finding 35
**Type:** Coherence — semantic overlap
**Requirements:** TECH-SERIAL-001, TECH-SERIAL-003
**Issue:** Both requirements specify the 9600-baud bit-period value. TECH-SERIAL-001 references `BIT_PERIOD = (1 000 000 / 9600) − 25` in `SoftSerial.cpp`; TECH-SERIAL-003 defines `DelaySerialBitPeriod()` with the same value. TECH-SERIAL-004 removes `BIT_PERIOD` from production code, making the TECH-SERIAL-001 source reference stale. Risk: updating the baud rate in one requirement without updating the other.
**Status:** open

### Finding 36
**Type:** Coherence — semantic overlap
**Requirements:** PLAT-POWER-001, PLAT-POWER-003
**Issue:** Both requirements describe behavior inside `Attiny84aHal::Hibernate()`. PLAT-POWER-001 covers the loop count; PLAT-POWER-003 covers the register operations per iteration. The overlap in scope (both describe `sleep()` internals) blurs each requirement's boundary.
**Status:** open

### Finding 37
**Type:** Coherence — precision
**Requirement:** FUNC-OREGON-010
**Issue:** States "values greater than `0xA5` are accepted without validation" but does not specify what happens to such values — whether the raw byte is written as-is, masked, or produces undefined behavior. Two developers could implement this differently. Behavior for out-of-range inputs is not independently verifiable.
**Status:** open

### Finding 38
**Type:** Coherence — precision
**Requirement:** FUNC-SENSOR-003
**Issue:** Specifies a 150 ms wait after BH1750 initialisation but does not state which HAL method or mechanism provides this delay. No `Hal` method for 150 ms exists in any requirement. `TECH-HAL-001` forbids direct `_delay_ms` outside `Attiny84aHal`, making the requirement untestable on host as written.
**Status:** open

### Finding 39
**Type:** Coherence — precision / conflict
**Requirements:** FUNC-X10-005, PLAT-POWER-004
**Issue:** FUNC-X10-005 states `LedOn`/`LedOff` bracket the full repeated sequence *inside* `x10rf::SendCommand()`. PLAT-POWER-004 states `TRANSMIT_WITH_LED` wraps *every* `Send()` call in `main.cpp`. If both are implemented, LED is toggled redundantly (once inside x10rf, once by main). The requirements conflict on LED ownership for X10 transmissions.
**Status:** open

### Finding 40
**Type:** Coherence — precision
**Requirements:** FUNC-LACROSSE-008, FUNC-LACROSSE-009
**Issue:** FUNC-LACROSSE-008 describes the two checksum nibbles as the final frame elements but does not confirm whether the trailing one-bit separator (FUNC-LACROSSE-009) applies to them. A developer reading only FUNC-LACROSSE-008 cannot determine this. The two requirements must be read together to derive correct behavior.
**Status:** open

### Finding 41
**Type:** Coherence — architecture mismatch
**Requirement:** FUNC-SENSOR-003
**Issue:** BH1750 sensor management is assigned to `main.cpp` without a dedicated wrapper class. This breaks the encapsulation pattern used by `BMx280` (FUNC-SENSOR-001) and `Ds18b20` (TECH-SENSOR-001), where all hardware interactions are in a class receiving `Hal*` at construction. BH1750 responsibility is misplaced.
**Status:** open
