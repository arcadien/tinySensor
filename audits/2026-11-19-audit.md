## Findings

### Finding 1
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirement:** TECH-HAL-004
**Issue:** The text says PA5 (MISO) only reaches the ISP header J6 and "pad 3 of the BME280 address jumper JP6 (open with the default 1–2 bridge)". On v3.0, JP6 is delivered open and one side is bridged at assembly (WIRING.md Jumpers table, BOM.md); with 2-3, PA5 is wired to the sensor SDO. PLAT-POWER-003 already allows for that. The reason given for leaving PA5 without a pull-up assumes the 1-2 bridge.
**Status:** open

### Finding 2
**Type:** Coherence — precision (C2)
**Requirement:** PLAT-POWER-007
**Issue:** The requirement states a rule for every board ("Every sensor board pulls its sensor buses … up with external resistors") but its own text says only the v3.0 schematic was checked. The mushroom runs a cut v2.2 board; no doc records which pull-ups a v2.2 board has or which rail they go to, so a v2.2 board cannot be judged against it. Either limit the scope to v3.0 or record the v2.2 pull-ups and rail.
**Status:** open

### Finding 3
**Type:** Coherence — architecture coherence (C5)
**Requirement:** TECH-HAL-004
**Issue:** The `docs/architecture.md` traceability row lists the power reduction, `PORTA = 0` and the PB2/PB3 pull-ups, but not the PA5 digital input buffer disable (`DIDR0` bit `ADC5D`) that the requirement now includes.
**Status:** open

### Finding 4
**Type:** Documentation — wrong
**Files:** hardware/tinysensor/WIRING.md:19 and :36, hardware/tinysensor/VARIANTE_LM35.md:117
**Issue:** "AIN (PA0, through R4 75 Ω)": in the netlist, J7.2, R4.1 and U1.13 are one net (`/Analog_sensor`), so PA0 is direct and R4 runs from PA0 to C3/JP3. "JP3 closed: 1 µF from PA0 to GND, RC filter with R4" is the opposite: JP3 closed shorts C3 and leaves R4 from PA0 to GND (LDR, negative LM35); the R4 + C3 filter exists only with JP3 open. README.md:129 and :134 describe it correctly.
**Status:** open

### Finding 5

**Status:** open
